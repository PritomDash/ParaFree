Useful for rough, quick rewrites where natural language is not the priority. Not recommended for academic, professional, or publication-quality writing. Free version has usage limits and ads. No document upload.

Paraphrasing and Academic Integrity

Paraphrasing is a legitimate academic skill — restating an idea in your own words to demonstrate understanding. It's taught in every academic writing program. The ethical question is not whether you use a tool, but how you use it.

Ethical uses of paraphrasing tools:

  • Rewriting your own draft to improve clarity and flow
  • Paraphrasing a source you are citing in your bibliography, to show you understood it
  • Improving fluency in a second language while keeping your original ideas
  • Practising how to express academic concepts in different words

Not ethical:

  • Submitting paraphrased content from a source without citing it
  • Rewriting someone else's essay and presenting it as your original work

A paraphrasing tool helps you express ideas more clearly — academic honesty is always your responsibility. Cite your sources.
