Frequently asked questions

Are these PDF tools really free?

Yes. All five tools are free, with no signup, no watermark and no daily cap on how many files you convert. ParaFree is kept running by donations.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Every tool runs inside your browser, so your files are opened and processed on your own device and are never sent to ParaFree or anyone else. The first time you open a tool, the page downloads the free open-source code it needs from the jsDelivr CDN — your files are not part of that.

Will my PDF look the same in Word?

Text, headings, bold and italic, lists and images are kept, and you can edit the result before downloading it. Complex layouts such as multi-column pages, tables and forms may not match the original exactly, so check the file before you submit it.

Can it convert scanned PDFs?

Yes. When a page has no text layer, for example a scan or photo of your notes, the PDF to Word tool reads it with OCR (text recognition) in your browser. OCR is most accurate on clear, straight English text, so read through the result before you use it.

How much smaller will my PDF get?

It depends on the file. Light mode keeps text selectable and shrinks large images, which helps most with PDFs full of photos or scans. Strong mode turns each page into an image for the smallest file, but the text can no longer be selected. If a PDF is already small, the tool tells you and you can keep the original.

Are there any file limits?

It depends on the tool and the browser. On desktop Chrome or Edge, Merge and Light compression take files up to 2 GB (Merge up to 4 GB per task), Strong and Target size compression, PDF to JPG and JPG to PDF up to 1 GB, PDF to Word up to 100 MB and Word to PDF up to 50 MB. Other browsers take up to 500 MB (Merge 1 GB per task) and 50 MB for PDF to Word and Word to PDF. PDF to Word handles up to 50 pages at a time, Merge and Light compression up to 2,000, the other tools up to 300.
