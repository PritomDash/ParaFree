Why So Many CV Builders Require a Signup

Creating an account is almost never about saving your progress — it's a business model. Here's what's really happening when a CV builder asks for your email:

  • You become a lead in their sales funnel. Expect emails pushing you to upgrade to a paid plan. Your email address has value to the company.
  • They can limit what you access until you pay. Account creation is a gate. The free tier often blocks PDF download, template switching, or AI features until you subscribe.
  • Your CV data is stored on their servers. This creates a data privacy risk. CV data is sensitive — it contains your employment history, address, phone number and sometimes salary information.
  • Deleting your account doesn't guarantee deletion of your data. Most privacy policies allow retention of some data even after account deletion.

ParaFree's business model is different. We're funded through the use of our broader AI writing tools (paraphraser, humanizer, AI chat assistant), not CV builder subscriptions. That's why we have no reason to gate the CV builder behind a login.

How ParaFree Protects Your Privacy

1. Your CV data never leaves your device

When you type your name, employment history, or contact details into ParaFree's CV builder, that data is saved in your browser's localStorage — a storage area on your own device. It is not transmitted to ParaFree's servers at any point.

2. No account means no email marketing

Without an email address, there is no way to send you upgrade emails, promotional offers, or marketing campaigns. ParaFree simply cannot contact you — because we don't know who you are.

3. AI features work without a login

The AI writing features (bullet points, professional summary, cover letter) do use an API call to generate content. That call includes only the text you've entered for that specific section — not your full CV, not identifying information, and not your email or identity.

4. Clearing your browser removes all data

If you want to completely remove your CV data, clear your browser's local storage for parafree.app. There's nothing on a server to chase down. Your data vanishes instantly.
