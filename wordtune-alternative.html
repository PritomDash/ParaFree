What Wordtune Does Well

Wordtune has a genuinely strong sentence-rewriting interface. It's worth acknowledging this clearly before talking about alternatives:

  • Multiple phrasing suggestions side by side — Wordtune shows you several alternative wordings at once, which is excellent for sentence-level editing decisions
  • Tone options — casual and formal modes let you adjust the register of individual sentences
  • Browser extension — integrates directly into Google Docs and Gmail, which is convenient for in-context editing
  • Spices feature — adds examples, statistics, and counterarguments to sentences (paid feature, but genuinely useful)

If you primarily want sentence-level suggestions with a polished UI, Wordtune's paid plan is worth considering.

Where Wordtune's Free Plan Falls Short

The free plan limitations are straightforward:

  • Daily rewrite limit — Wordtune's free plan limits the number of rewrites you can run per day. Once you hit the limit, you need to wait until the next day or upgrade.
  • Signup required — you need to create an account to use Wordtune, even on the free plan
  • No document upload on free plan — uploading Word documents or PDFs requires a paid subscription
  • No PowerPoint support — Wordtune does not paraphrase PowerPoint files
