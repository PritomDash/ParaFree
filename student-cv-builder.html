What to Put on a Student CV with No Work Experience

The biggest mistake students make is leaving sections blank because they think they have "nothing to put." Here's what actually counts as experience for a first CV:

Education

Your degree, institution, expected graduation year, and any relevant modules. Include your grade or predicted class if strong.

Academic Projects

Dissertations, group research projects, coursework deliverables, lab work. Describe the problem you solved and the skills you used.

Part-Time Work

Retail, hospitality, tutoring, delivery — any paid work demonstrates reliability, time management and customer-facing communication.

Volunteering

Charity work, community events, student union roles. Shows initiative and values beyond academic achievement.

Societies & Sports

Society committee roles, sports team captaincy, event organising — all demonstrate leadership, teamwork and commitment.

Technical Skills

Software, programming languages, lab techniques, design tools, languages. Include proficiency level (basic/intermediate/advanced).

Student CV Format: What Recruiters Expect

For most entry-level and graduate roles, recruiters expect:

  • Length: one page. As a student or recent graduate, you very rarely need two pages. A tight, well-written one-page CV signals good judgment and communication skills.
  • Order: reverse chronological for education and experience. Most recent first in each section.
  • Contact information at the top. Name, phone number, professional email (firstname.lastname@gmail.com, not something embarrassing), LinkedIn URL if well-maintained.
  • Professional summary: 2–3 sentences. Your degree level, subject, expected graduation, and 1–2 key qualities or career interests.
  • Education before experience for students with limited work history. Once you have 2+ years of relevant experience, flip the order.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I write a CV when I have no work experience?

Focus on academic projects, coursework, volunteer work, societies, sports leadership and any part-time jobs. ParaFree's AI can take rough notes about these and write them as professional bullet points demonstrating transferable skills.

What should a student put on their first CV?

Contact information, a short professional summary, education (with relevant modules and predicted grade), any work experience, key projects or dissertations, skills (technical and soft), and activities. A well-written one page covering these is professionally strong.

Is ParaFree's student CV builder free?

How long should a student CV be?

One page is standard for student and graduate CVs in the UK, Australia and most international markets. Only expand to two pages if you have substantial relevant experience (typically 3+ years post-graduation).

Should I include my grades on my student CV?

Include your grade if it's strong — typically a 2:1 or above (UK), GPA 3.0+ (US), or a Credit average or higher (Australia). If lower but improving, you may omit grades and focus on experience instead.
