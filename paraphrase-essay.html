What Makes Good Essay Paraphrasing

Not all paraphrasing tools handle essays well. Many work sentence-by-sentence, replacing words with synonyms. The result reads as robotic and unnatural because the sentences no longer flow together as a coherent argument.

ParaFree uses meaning-based AI that reads the paragraph as a whole before rewriting. This means:

  • Argument flow is maintained — your essay's logical structure survives the rewrite
  • Transitions work — "however", "therefore", "in contrast" are used where they make sense, not randomly
  • No factual errors introduced — the AI rewrites expression, not your claims
  • Natural English — reads like a person wrote it, not a thesaurus

Academic Integrity — Read This

Paraphrasing is a core academic skill: restating an idea in your own words to demonstrate understanding. ParaFree is designed to help you express ideas more clearly.

Ethical uses of essay paraphrasing:

  • Rewriting your own draft for greater clarity and fluency
  • Paraphrasing a source you are citing in your bibliography to demonstrate understanding
  • Improving fluency in a second language while keeping your original ideas
  • Practicing how to express academic concepts in different words

Not ethical:

  • Submitting paraphrased content without attribution to the original source
  • Rewriting someone else's essay and claiming it as your own work

The tool improves expression — academic honesty is your responsibility. Always cite your sources.
